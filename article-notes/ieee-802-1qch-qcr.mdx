## IEEE 802.1Qch: Cyclic Queuing and Forwarding

[IEEE 802.1Qch](https://1.ieee802.org/tsn/802-1qch/) chooses the **output queue of a frame from the time it arrives**:

- Each port keeps at least **two queues** that are enabled in turn by a periodic schedule (Qbv gates). At any time one queue sends while the other collects.
- Frames received during a cycle go into the queue that is currently closed. When the queues swap, everything collected is sent and new arrivals go to the other queue.
- So a frame spends about **one cycle per hop**, and the network transit latency is characterised by the cycle time (YSH19 §4.2). The original name was the **peristaltic shaper**.
- The aim is to minimise **relay jitter**.
- The cycle split and the change of internal priority reuse the stream gates of [802.1Qci](./ieee-802-1qci.mdx) at ingress (LS19 §IV-D).

LS19 saw no strong sign in 2019 that Qch would be adopted in industry. The [CQF post](/blog/cyclic-queuing-and-forwarding) gives the cycle-time condition that makes the per-hop bound hold.

## IEEE 802.1Qcr: Asynchronous Traffic Shaping

[IEEE 802.1Qcr](https://1.ieee802.org/tsn/802-1qcr/) gives guaranteed low latency **without a synchronised time base**. It adds a layer of **shaped queues** at egress, after the urgency-based scheduler of Specht and Samii (LS19 §IV-G).

- Unlike Qbv, it needs neither clocks nor a schedule; unlike CBS, it shapes **per stream** (or per group of streams), which keeps bursts from building up across hops.
- LS19 expected it to matter mostly for the **automotive** industry, given who contributed to the project.

It was still a project (P802.1Qcr) when LS19 was written; it was published as [802.1Qcr-2020](https://1.ieee802.org/tsn/802-1qcr/). The [ATS post](/blog/asynchronous-traffic-shaping) covers the token-bucket shapers, the queue allocation rules and the shaper-to-shaper delay segments.

## Where they sit among the shapers

| Mechanism | Needs synchronised time | Unit of shaping | Typical use (LS19 view) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [CBS (Qav)](./ieee-802-1qav.mdx) | No | Traffic class | Audio/video streams |
| [TAS (Qbv)](./ieee-802-1qbv.mdx) | Yes | Queue, by gate schedule | Time-triggered control |
| CQF (Qch) | Yes | Cycle | Low relay jitter, simple configuration |
| ATS (Qcr) | No | Stream or stream group | Automotive, asynchronous critical traffic |
