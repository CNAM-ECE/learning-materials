# Ethics of Artificial Intelligence

A six-hour seminar on the ethical and societal dimensions of artificial intelligence. It is scheduled as the second day of the two-part ethics programme described in ADUM and is mandatory in the supplied schedule.

## What the course covers

- Defining AI, distinguishing technical realities from popular narratives, and understanding AI as a social construction.
- AI paradigms and the scope and limits of current systems.
- Societal impacts: digital divides, data colonialism, inequality and algorithmic discrimination, misinformation, surveillance, privacy, persuasion, geopolitics, environmental effects and generative AI.
- Ethical principles, including beneficence, non-maleficence, autonomy, justice, explainability, transparency, accountability, fairness, privacy and safety.
- International, European and national approaches to AI governance, including the GDPR, EU AI Act, UNESCO recommendations and ISO/IEC 42001.
- Critical examination of algorithmic bias and claims of technological neutrality.
