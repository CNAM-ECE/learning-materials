# Introduction to Bibliometrics

A three-hour, French-language online workshop for Cnam doctoral candidates on bibliometric tools and their use in research evaluation.

## What the course covers

The session introduces the history and applications of bibliometrics, key indicators and tools, how to select an appropriate tool, and critiques and recommendations. It also considers the influence of open science, alternative metrics and the limitations of quantitative evaluation. Participants practise basic navigation in Scopus.

By the end, participants should be able to explain major indicators and tools, recognise their uses and limits, identify alternative metrics and use basic Scopus features.
