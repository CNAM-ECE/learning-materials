# Innovation Processes

A two-day, French-language course on contemporary innovation, combining taught sessions, museum-based exploration and case studies. Its stated objective is to help doctoral candidates understand innovation processes and develop the ability to analyse and act on innovation situations.

## What the course covers

- Concepts, challenges and processes of contemporary innovation, including the idea of disruptive innovation.
- Innovation in historical and current professional contexts, considering both successes and failures.
- Strategies for developing and implementing innovation, and critical analysis of real cases.
- A guided exploration of technical objects at the Musée des arts et métiers, followed by two case studies. The programme also points to the FUN/Cnam MOOC *Fabriquer l’innovation* as further study.
