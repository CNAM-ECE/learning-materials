# J1A Cnam — Welcome Seminar

A French-language, in-person introduction to Cnam and its Sciences des Métiers de l’Ingénieur (SMI) doctoral school. The seminar is for doctoral candidates enrolled at Cnam; it is mandatory for first-year SMI doctoral candidates enrolled at Cnam.

## What the course covers

The programme introduces the institution and doctoral-school support, including the research library, student wellbeing and anti-violence support, Erasmus+, and doctoral experience. The day concludes with visits to learning and research spaces at the Landy site, including the Fab Lab and immersive-technology workshops.
