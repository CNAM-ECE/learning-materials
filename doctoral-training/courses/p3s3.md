# P3S3 — Privacy, Protocols & Proofs

An international five-day summer school for doctoral candidates and early-career researchers, held at Institut Pascal in Orsay. It combines advanced cryptography, formal security analysis and privacy-enhancing technologies (PETs), with lectures and a research poster session.

## What the course covers

- **Cryptographic protocols:** zero-knowledge proofs, homomorphic encryption and electronic voting; constructions including Schnorr proofs, IOP/PIOP and polynomial commitments.
- **Privacy-enhancing technologies:** technical and legal perspectives, including the trade-offs among privacy, utility and computational cost.
- **Formal security methods:** protocol verification using Tamarin; information-flow analysis, including static analysis, dynamic monitoring, non-interference and preservation through compilation.
- **Legal context:** GDPR and European digital identity wallets.
- **Research communication:** presenting a poster to an international, interdisciplinary audience.

The programme develops skills in evaluating modern privacy technologies, specifying and formally analysing security properties, situating technical choices in their legal context, and communicating research. Participants can connect these methods to their own research questions.
