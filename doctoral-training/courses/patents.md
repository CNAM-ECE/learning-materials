# Invention Patents — INPI

A four-hour, French-language online course introducing intellectual property and the protection of inventions. It is listed under professional development and career preparation.

## What the course covers

**Day 1 — Intellectual-property foundations:** purposes and branches of IP; patents, trademarks, designs and models; complementary protections such as know-how, trade secrets and the Soleau envelope; the roles of INPI, EPO, WIPO and EUIPO; territorial scope, duration and infringement.

**Day 2 — Patent practice:** patent rights and their limits; patentability criteria and exclusions; patent structure; the French filing process, search report, timelines and costs; European and international routes, including the unitary patent and PCT; filing strategy; licensing, assignment, enforcement and commercialisation.

Participants should leave able to distinguish the main forms of IP, understand basic patentability and filing in France, recognise international protection routes, and consider IP in research, innovation and professional practice.
