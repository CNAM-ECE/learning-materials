# Thesis in 180 Seconds Coaching

A French-language doctoral-college programme for researchers who want to explain their thesis in simple, concise terms to a broad audience, particularly in preparation for the *Ma thèse en 180 secondes* (MT180) competition.

## What the course covers

Two remote half-day seminars combine short theoretical inputs with small-group exercises and feedback:

1. Why communicate research to the public, and what can science communication achieve?
2. How to identify an audience and adapt a research message for it.

Participation may lead to involvement in the MT180 candidate selection on 14 January 2027. Selected candidates receive further small-group coaching for the competition. The course develops clear oral communication, audience awareness and collaborative practice; the detailed coaching plan is determined by the organisers.
