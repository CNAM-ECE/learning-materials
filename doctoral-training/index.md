# Doctoral training

The thirteen courses of the 2026–2027 doctoral training schedule at the [Cnam](https://www.cnam.fr/) doctoral school *Sciences des métiers de l'ingénieur* ([ED SMI](https://ed-smi.cnam.fr/)). Each course has a page with what it covers, its schedule and, once the session has taken place, the notes taken during it. The course descriptions come from the [CNAM-ECE/doctoral-training](https://github.com/CNAM-ECE/doctoral-training) repository, which follows the ADUM programme pages.

## Hours

The [schedule and hours tracker](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1Q3cOgzXp-fjgBAWXF3LVPOH4MeAEnVFNgJ9EkjaDLGA/edit?gid=759810514#gid=759810514) is the source of truth for dates, registration status and hours. At the last snapshot:

| Category | Hours |
| --- | ---: |
| Scientific | 54 |
| Professional | 50 |
| **Counted toward the training target** | **104** |
| Supplementary, not counted | 31 |

The minimum of 50 hours in each category is met. The totals change when a course status or a credit recognition is updated.

## How the notes are written

Notes are taken by hand during the session, then rewritten here as web pages: headings follow the order of the course, diagrams drawn on paper become lists or tables, and French terms are kept in parentheses where they are the ones used in the course. The scanned pages stay available at the bottom of each course page.
